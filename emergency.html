What O-SAAR Cannot Provide

Clarity protects you. O-SAAR is therapy support — not emergency response.

Not for

  • Emergency response
  • Hospital care
  • 24/7 crisis intervention
  • Medical prescriptions
  • Police assistance
  • Ambulance services

If you need any of these right now, please contact local emergency services or go to the nearest hospital. You are not alone — help is meant for moments like this.

A Gentle Safety Plan

Small steps that can help you feel a little safer, one moment at a time.

1 Take slow breaths.

Inhale gently through your nose, then exhale a little longer. There is no perfect way — only a softer rhythm.

2 Move to a safe place.

If you can, go somewhere that feels quieter and more protected — even a different room can help.

3 Contact someone you trust.

Reach out to a friend, family member, or someone who cares. You do not have to explain everything to ask for presence.

4 Remove anything dangerous nearby.

If it feels possible, create a safer environment around you. Your wellbeing matters more than anything else in this moment.

5 Call emergency services if necessary.

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services without delay. Asking for urgent help is an act of care.
